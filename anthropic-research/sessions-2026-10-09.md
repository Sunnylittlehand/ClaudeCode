OpenAI Editorial. The textGrain announcement comes from OpenAI's product and policy team as a direct response to EU AI Act Article 50 requirements, which mandate machine-readable marking of AI-generated text. OpenAI competes directly with Anthropic across enterprise AI and developer tooling; this move brings OpenAI into regulatory parity with Anthropic, which rolled out comparable watermarking globally in August 2026.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ — Direct competitive and regulatory development: OpenAI closes Anthropic's three-month watermarking lead, with Codex-specific implications for Sunny's regulated-industry rollout context.

### 摘要
OpenAI 于10月5日宣布 textGrain 系统：在ChatGPT 和 Codex 文本中嵌入统计隐形水印，以符合《欧盟人工智能法》第50条要求。初始阶段仅在欧盟范围内推送，API 开发者可全球选择性接入，但检测器暂限研究机构。OpenAI 坦承技术存在局限：替换25%词语可将检测率从92%降至17%。

### Summary
OpenAI's textGrain subtly alters word choices during generation to embed a statistical pattern that a detector can later identify — the same architectural approach as Anthropic's August 2026 watermarking rollout. OpenAI itself warns the system is imperfect: replacing 25% of words in a 400-token passage reduces detection from ~92% to ~17%.

### Main Takeaway
OpenAI has closed Anthropic's watermarking gap three months after it opened — both major coding-AI vendors now ship EU-compliant text provenance, raising the baseline expectation for enterprise AI procurement.
