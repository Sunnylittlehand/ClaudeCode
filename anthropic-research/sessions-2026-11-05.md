## [Anthropic FlashProve — Open-Source Cryptographic Proof of AI Model Identity](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-working-on-a-system-to-prove-ai-models-identity/)
**Source**: Anthropic
**Type**: Research Publication
**Date**: 2026-11-03
**Links**: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-working-on-a-system-to-prove-ai-models-identity/) · [Heise (technical)](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Who-s-talking-Anthropic-unveils-AI-model-identity-verification-11511181.html) · [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/articles/anthropics-flashprove-puts-ai-model-identity-to-a-cryptographic-test/)

### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Research team. Published November 3 as an open-source research prototype on GitHub. This is notably the first public artefact from Anthropic's provable-inference programme — a commitment in its Responsible Scaling Policy roadmap that had a September 30, 2026 deadline which passed without public acknowledgement. FlashProve represents Anthropic's belated but substantive first public response to that milestone, arriving during the IPO quiet period.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐ — A meaningful research prototype rather than a shipped product, but the first concrete implementation of Anthropic's long-delayed provable-inference commitment; the concept has direct governance and auditability implications for enterprise AI deployment.

### 摘要
Anthropic于11月3日发布开源研究原型FlashProve，使用密码学证明某一AI输出确实来自特定模型权重。核心机制：实验室公开模型"指纹"承诺，推理时生成可在本地CPU上17–21秒验证的证明；以Meta Llama 4 Maverick为例，H100 GPU批处理24条提示约需223秒生成证明。目前仅为研究阶段，Anthropic明确表示暂无部署计划，但技术可防止AI服务商悄悄以廉价模型替换高端模型。

### Summary
Anthropic released FlashProve, an open-source research prototype that generates a cryptographic proof that a given AI output came from a specific model's weights — allowing a third party to verify which model produced a response without the lab exposing its weights. Labs publish a commitment (a fingerprint tied to model weights) at training time; at inference, a proof is generated and verified client-side in 17–21 seconds on a CPU. In Anthropic's tests using Meta's Llama 4 Maverick, proof generation for a 24-prompt batch took 223 seconds on an H100 GPU. Anthropic describes it as an unmaintained research prototype with no current plans to deploy it at any lab.

### Main Takeaway
FlashProve answers a question that had gone unanswered since Anthropic's September 30 roadmap deadline: Anthropic now has working code — if not yet a deployed system — for cryptographic proof that a response came from the model the vendor claimed.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐⭐ — The underlying problem FlashProve addresses — proving which model actually produced an AI output — is directly relevant to audit and governance requirements in regulated financial services. FCA operational resilience rules require firms to understand the technology they depend on; if Anthropic (or any vendor) can silently swap in a cheaper model, that undermines the firm's ability to attest to what system processed a decision. FlashProve is currently a research prototype, not an enterprise feature, but its existence signals that Anthropic is building the infrastructure for model-identity attestation that enterprise buyers and regulators will eventually require. Sunny should track this for future contract language around model-identity guarantees.

## [Anthropic Claude Plugin Trusted Issuer — Enterprise Auto-Connect for 7 Workplace Plugins](https://thenewstack.io/claude-plugin-identity-issuer/)
**Source**: Anthropic
**Type**: Product Update
**Date**: 2026-11-03
**Links**: [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/claude-plugin-identity-issuer/) · [ClaudeDevs on X](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2117680635561312732) · [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/48715)

### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Editorial (Platform / Enterprise team). Announced via the official ClaudeDevs X account on November 3, 2026. Available in beta for Claude Team and Enterprise plans.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐ — A meaningful enterprise UX improvement that also introduces a material offboarding security gap; the gap is significant enough that regulated enterprises should review access revocation workflows before enabling it.

### 摘要
Anthropic为Team和Enterprise推出插件可信颁发机构功能：管理员预配置插件后，员工首次使用时Claude可自动完成连接，无需单独登录。目前支持7个连接器（Amplitude、Atlassian、Datadog、Figma、Linear、Notion、Supabase）。需注意：从企业身份提供商（如Okta）移除员工账户，不会自动撤销Claude颁发的Supabase等连接服务访问权限——需分别处理。

### Summary
Anthropic launched a Trusted Issuer beta for Team and Enterprise: when an admin pre-configures a supported plugin, employees connect automatically on first use — no separate OAuth sign-in required, even if the company's identity provider does not support the OIDC extension. The feature is live for seven of the fifteen connectors that support managed authorisation: Amplitude, Atlassian, Datadog, Figma, Linear, Notion, and Supabase. The critical offboarding gap: removing an employee from the company's identity provider (Okta/Entra ID) does not automatically revoke Claude-issued access to connected services — Supabase access, for example, persists until separately revoked.

### Main Takeaway
Anthropic is now an identity issuer for enterprise software — useful for onboarding, but it creates a new offboarding workflow that security teams must close before enabling.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐⭐⭐ — This is a direct enterprise deployment consideration. The auto-connect feature reduces friction for onboarding developers to Claude Code's plugin ecosystem — Atlassian (Jira/Confluence) and Datadog are likely already in use at Sunny's bank. But the offboarding gap is a material security issue in a regulated environment: when a developer leaves the bank, their Claude-issued access to Supabase or other connected services will not be revoked automatically by deprovisioning their corporate identity. Before the bank enables Trusted Issuer for any plugin, the deployment team must map which connectors are in scope and add an explicit Claude plugin revocation step to the joiner-mover-leaver process. This should be documented in the bank's Claude Code governance policy before the feature is switched on.

OpenAI Editorial (Research & Product). Launched October 30, 2026 — the same week as GPT-6 rolled out to all ChatGPT users. This is a distinct product from the May 2026 Rosalind Biodefense launch; that was a narrowly scoped biosecurity tool, whereas Rosalind Research is a full frontier reasoning model for broader life sciences R&D.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐ — A meaningful vertical-specific model launch that shows OpenAI's pattern of building trusted-access tiers for high-stakes domains; the access-governance model it uses is worth watching as a template for future regulated-industry expansions.

### 摘要
OpenAI于10月30日发布GPT-6 Rosalind Research，这是基于GPT-6 Astra权重的生命科学推理模型，面向经审核的研究机构开放。访问分级：获批研究机构可在ChatGPT Work和Codex中使用，API访问需单独审批。上线两周内提供管理员预览，默认关闭。LifeSciBench Gold评测：GPT-Rosalind-6得分61.3%，GPT-Rosalind-5.5得分44.0%。部分双重用途主题（GPT-6 Astra通常拒绝回答）在此模型中被允许。

### Summary
OpenAI launched GPT-6 Rosalind Research, a frontier reasoning model on GPT-6 Astra weights fine-tuned for biology, drug discovery, and translational medicine, available to approved enterprise and research organisations in Work and Codex. Access requires a two-step approval (organisation-level and user-level) with a two-week admin preview period where the model is off by default; API access requires separate approval. On OpenAI's LifeSciBench Gold evaluation, GPT-Rosalind-6 scores 61.3% versus 44.0% for GPT-Rosalind-5.5. The model relaxes some dual-use biology restrictions that GPT-6 Astra normally refuses, available only to verified organisations with appropriate research governance.

### Main Takeaway
OpenAI's trusted-access tier model — applied now to legal (Astra for Law), semiconductor (GPT-Synopsys), and life sciences (Rosalind Research) — is becoming a template for how frontier AI enters regulated industries without blanket access.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐ — This is primarily competitive context rather than direct action.
