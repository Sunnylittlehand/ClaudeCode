### Summary
On 6 October 2026, Anthropic launched Claude for Google Workspace in public beta for all paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise). A sidebar add-on installed via the Google Workspace Marketplace brings Claude into Docs, Sheets, and Slides; the default "Ask before edits" mode requires user approval for each change, while an "Accept all edits" mode lets Claude proceed to completion. Enterprise admins can deploy via Google Admin Console to specific domains or groups, and existing Compliance API, customer-managed encryption keys, and OpenTelemetry audit-export controls extend to the add-on. A companion connector (also beta) runs in the other direction — letting Claude create and edit Google files from the Claude chat interface.
