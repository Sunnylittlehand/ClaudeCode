### 摘要
Anthropic于2026年10月6日宣布扩展并重构其网络安全验证计划，将此前的单级"网络验证计划"与精英项目"Project Glasswing"合并为一套三层访问体系。三个层级分别为：防御访问（逆向工程恶意软件、事件响应等防御性工作）、红队访问（授权系统的渗透测试）和专项访问（针对生命安全与市场稳定等高风险系统，限制最少）。可访问的模型包括Claude Mythos 5.1、Opus 5.5和Sonnet 5.5。符合条件的申请者涵盖企业安全团队、大学、政府机构、关键基础设施运营商及独立安全研究人员。Anthropic承诺在数日内完成申请审核。

### Summary
On October 6, Anthropic merged its original Cyber Verification Program with Project Glasswing into a single three-tier framework. The three tiers are: **Defense Access** (defensive work such as malware reverse-engineering, incident response, and vulnerability analysis); **Red Team Access** (offensive testing within authorised systems, with real-time safeguards blocking ransomware deployment and attacks on high-risk safety systems); and **Specialized Access** (the fewest cyber restrictions, aimed at systems where failures could threaten lives or disrupt markets). All three tiers cover Claude Mythos 5.1, Opus 5.5, and Sonnet 5.5, plus future models. Eligible applicants include corporate security teams, universities, government bodies, critical infrastructure operators, smaller security firms, open-source maintainers, and individual researchers with established CVE-reporting histories. Anthropic aims to respond to applications within a few days.

### Main Takeaway
