### Significance Rating

### 摘要
OpenAI 宣布 ChatGPT for Healthcare 与 Epic EHR 系统对接，后者覆盖美国约 40% 的医院。临床医生可在 ChatGPT 对话中调阅已授权的病历数据（预约记录、化验结果、用药信息等），也可直接在 Epic 界面内嵌入 ChatGPT，无需切换系统。该集成为只读模式，签署 BAA 协议的机构可获 HIPAA 合规保障。同步上线的还有 Healthcare Public Data 插件，接入 ClinicalTrials.gov、CMS、RxNorm、DailyMed 及 PubMed 等公共数据源。

### Summary
OpenAI connected ChatGPT for Healthcare to Epic EHR — which powers clinical operations at approximately 40% of US hospitals — giving clinicians read-only access to authorised patient records (notes, labs, medications, specialist documentation) either within a ChatGPT conversation or embedded directly inside Epic's interface. Organisations that have signed a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with OpenAI can use this in compliance-sensitive workflows; Codex is also covered by the BAA. A companion Healthcare Public Data plugin taps ClinicalTrials.gov, CMS Coverage, RxNorm, DailyMed, and PubMed for trial eligibility, drug identifiers, and coverage policies. Launch partners include AdventHealth, Boston Children's Hospital, Cedars-Sinai, and UCSF.

### Main Takeaway
OpenAI's Epic integration is the clearest template yet for how a frontier AI vendor structures a regulated-industry product: read-only, BAA-covered, embedded in existing clinical workflows — and it raises the bar for what banks and regulated-sector clients will expect from any comparable Claude integration.
