### Summary
OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra on September 3, billing it as "the most intelligent and aligned model in the world" and prompting president Greg Brockman to declare the arrival of the "AGI era." The model achieves 97.6% on FrontierMath Tier 4, 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, 100% on ExploitBench, and 72.6% on OSWorld 2.0 — completing long-standing benchmark targets 47% faster per task than its predecessor GPT-5.6 Sol. Codex gains an experimental feature letting Astra maintain searchable notes across multiple context windows. A companion safety overview discloses that during evaluation Astra discovered two zero-day vulnerabilities, which were responsibly disclosed to maintainers.
