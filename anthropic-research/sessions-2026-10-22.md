### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Editorial. Claude Duet is Anthropic's first always-on agent product, representing a significant architectural and commercial shift: from a reactive chatbot to a proactive agent that initiates contact and runs continuously in the background.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — A purpose-built proactive agent app built on Anthropic's most capable model positions Anthropic directly against OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse, marking the opening of a new competitive front in always-on personal AI agents.

### 摘要
Anthropic 于 10 月 21 日正式推出 Claude Duet，这是其首款主动式 AI 代理应用，目前以研究预览版形式对美国 Pro 和 Max 订阅用户开放（须加入候补名单）。Duet 基于 Claude Opus 5.5 构建，运行在 Anthropic 的云端计算机上，即使用户关闭应用后任务仍可持续执行。它会主动监控已连接的应用（每隔数小时扫描一次），提供晨间简报，并在需要用户注意时主动推送提醒。在执行发送消息、付款、签署文件或删除文件等敏感操作前，Duet 会先征得用户批准。团队版与企业版暂未纳入当前阶段。

### Summary
Anthropic launched Claude Duet on October 21, 2026 as a research preview, accessible to US Pro and Max subscribers via a waitlist on iOS, Android, and the web. Built on Claude Opus 5.5, it runs on its own cloud computer so tasks continue after the user closes the app. Duet sends a morning brief and proactively alerts users when connected apps need attention, checking those services every few hours. Before taking consequential actions — sending messages, making payments, signing documents, or deleting files — it asks for explicit user approval. Team and Enterprise plans are not yet included; Anthropic gave no timeline for expansion. Background work counts against existing plan usage limits at no extra charge, but Anthropic acknowledges the agent can misjudge urgency and will miss items that arrive between check-ins.

### Main Takeaway
Claude Duet is Anthropic's opening move in the always-on personal agent race — a direct answer to OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse, built on its flagship model and designed around a human-approval loop for sensitive actions.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — This is high-priority competitive and enterprise-strategy intelligence for Sunny's rollout. First, the competitive picture: Anthropic is now shipping the same category of always-on proactive agent as OpenAI (Dots) and Meta (Muse), which will accelerate enterprise demand for agentic AI and raise expectations among the developer teams Sunny is onboarding. Second, the approval-loop design — Duet asks before sending messages, making payments, or deleting files — sets a precedent for what responsible enterprise agentic AI looks like and gives Sunny a template to reference in governance discussions. Third, the current exclusion of Team and Enterprise plans is notable: Anthropic is validating safety and UX in the consumer tier before releasing to regulated enterprise — a sensible sequencing that Sunny should communicate to internal stakeholders who might wonder why they don't have access yet. When Duet does extend to Enterprise, the bank's security teams will need to review which apps are connected and what data flows through the cloud computer, particularly given the bank's data-residency and outbound-transfer policies.
