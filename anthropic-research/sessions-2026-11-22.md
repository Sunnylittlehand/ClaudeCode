## [Anthropic "Worse Together" — Multi-User Multi-Agent Study Shows Coordination Failure Risk](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/worse-together/)
**Source**: Anthropic
**Type**: Research / Alignment Science Blog Post
**Date**: 2026-11-11
**Links**: [Alignment Science Blog](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/worse-together/) · [MAMUBench benchmark release](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/worse-together/)

### Speaker / Author
Sahan Paliskara, Andrew Lampinen, and co-authors — Anthropic Fellows Program. The Anthropic Fellows Program funds embedded safety and alignment research. Andrew Lampinen is a senior research scientist at Anthropic with a background in cognitive science and multi-agent learning, known for work on how agents generalise across contexts.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Directly quantifies a governance failure mode that every enterprise deploying multi-agent AI faces: individual agents optimise for their own user at the expense of group outcomes, and this risk is not visible from single-agent safety evals.

### 摘要
Anthropic 研究发现，当每位用户都拥有独立 AI 代理时，团队整体决策质量反而低于由单一协调代理服务所有用户的方案——即"越多反而越差"。研究测试了四种配置：独立代理、统一协调者、静默团队（无通信）和点对点团队（有通信渠道）。在所有环境中，个人代理模式带来更差的集体结果。团队同时发布 MAMUBench 基准，供外部研究者评测多用户多代理系统的协调行为与对齐表现。

### Summary
Anthropic's alignment researchers tested four multi-user, multi-agent configurations and found that group outcomes were consistently worse when each user had their own personal agent compared to a single coordinator serving multiple users. The study tested a solo agent, a coordinator, a silent team (agents with no inter-agent communication), and a peer-to-peer team (agents with a shared channel). The finding holds across all three environments released as the MAMUBench benchmark. The authors conclude that single-agent alignment results do not generalise to multi-agent deployments.

### Main Takeaway
"Worse together" — each user getting their own AI agent systematically degrades group-level outcomes, a failure mode that single-agent safety evals cannot detect.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Must-read for the Claude Code enterprise rollout. Sunny's developers will increasingly use multi-agent Claude Code workflows (teams of subagents, parallel coding sessions). This study gives her a concrete, Anthropic-sourced argument for why governance of multi-agent deployments cannot be inferred from single-agent testing — she needs separate multi-agent evaluation criteria for FCA/PRA auditors.
