### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Editorial, with reporting attributed to Bloomberg correspondents with direct source access. The news represents a formal policy change coordinated with more than 100 enterprise customers including Salesforce, signalling it has executive sponsorship at the highest level.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — A direct policy reversal on the single biggest enterprise adoption blocker for Anthropic's most capable models, arriving one day after OpenAI announced ZDR for frontier models, and likely to reshape regulated-industry vendor conversations immediately.

### 摘要
Anthropic 宣布将允许企业客户将 Fable 和 Mythos 等前沿模型所要求的 30 天安全数据留存迁移至客户自有云基础设施，不再强制存储在 Anthropic 服务器上。这一变化回应了百余家企业客户的强烈反弹，并与 Salesforce 等重要客户历时数月协调制定而成。新的安全系统将于 2026 年底前推出，留存时限维持 30 天不变，但数据主权归属彻底转变。在 OpenAI 于 8 月 19 日宣布零数据留存（ZDR）的同日之后，此举的竞争战略意义极为显著，对受监管行业的买家尤其如此。

### Summary
Anthropic will allow enterprise customers to store the mandatory 30-day safety-retention window for their Fable and Mythos model traffic on their own cloud infrastructure, rather than on Anthropic's servers. The 30-day retention period itself is unchanged — what changes is where the data sits. The new system, coordinated with over 100 customers including Salesforce over several months, is expected to roll out later in 2026. The announcement arrives one day after OpenAI debuted Zero Data Retention (ZDR), directly addressing the competitive gap Axios had flagged as favouring OpenAI for regulated-industry buyers.

### Main Takeaway
Anthropic matches OpenAI's enterprise data-sovereignty challenge by shifting mandatory 30-day safety retention to customer-controlled cloud storage — a pivotal move for banks and financial services firms.

The previous policy — Anthropic retaining all Fable/Mythos traffic for 30 days on its own servers — was the single biggest blocker for UK bank vendor-risk assessments under FCA CP22/25 data-residency guidance and PRA SS2/21 outsourcing rules, because it meant a mandatory transfer of sensitive prompt data to a US third-party cloud.
