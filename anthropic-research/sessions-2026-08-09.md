The timing — one day before EU AI Act fining powers went live on August 1 — signals that this was a deliberate regulatory positioning move rather than a routine feature release.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ — OpenAI's first audio content-provenance layer, timed precisely to EU AI Act enforcement; introduces a cross-vendor verification standard relevant to regulated-industry AI workflows.

### 摘要
OpenAI 于 2026 年 7 月 31 日（欧盟《人工智能法》正式生效前一天）为 GPT-Live 语音模型推出了双层音频溯源体系：内嵌 SynthID 不可见水印（即使剪辑、压缩后仍可检测）以及 C2PA 内容凭证（记录完整创作历史）。同时推出公共验证工具 "Verify"，允许任意人上传音频文件核验是否由 OpenAI 生成；并开放 API 端点供企业集成至自有合规工作流。

### Summary
OpenAI has embedded SynthID watermarks — an invisible, compression-resistant audio fingerprint developed with Google DeepMind — into all audio generated by GPT-Live through ChatGPT Voice and the API, paired with C2PA content credentials that record the full creation chain. A public web tool called Verify allows anyone to upload an audio file and check for OpenAI provenance signals, and a Content Provenance API lets organisations query provenance programmatically. The announcement was timed to land one day before EU AI Act enforcement and fining powers began on August 1, 2026, signalling a deliberate regulatory-compliance framing. SynthID survives format conversion and lossy compression while C2PA carries richer but strippable metadata; the dual-layer design hedges against either layer's weaknesses.

### Main Takeaway
OpenAI has given AI-generated audio a verifiable identity — one day before EU law required it — and opened that verification to API integration, setting the floor for audio provenance across the industry.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ — High value — direct implications for compliance and vendor evaluation. Any AI tool generating audio in a regulated context (call-centre automation, client communications, meeting transcription with AI-synthesised summaries) now faces a de facto industry expectation that the output carries provenance metadata. The EU AI Act's Chapter III transparency obligations for AI-generated content apply to Anthropic's tools as much as OpenAI's; Sunny should check whether Claude Voice and Claude Cowork's audio outputs carry equivalent provenance.
