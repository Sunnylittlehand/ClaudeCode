## [Claude Achieves a Mathematical Breakthrough on the Riemann Zeta Function — Lower Bound Raised from 41.6% to 67.2%](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta)
**Source**: Anthropic
**Type**: Research Paper / Technical Blog Post
**Date**: August 10, 2026
**Links**: [Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta) · [AlphaSignal coverage](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-pushes-a-160-year-old-math-boundary-from-41-6-to-67-2)

### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Research. The result was produced by an unreleased research version of Claude operating as a multi-agent swarm inside Claude Code, then formally verified by Lean and reviewed by external number theorists Brian Conrey (American Institute of Mathematics) and Dan Goldston (San Jose State University) — two of the world's leading experts on the distribution of Riemann zeta zeros.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — The first time an AI system has made a materially novel and independently verified advance on a 160-year-old open problem in pure mathematics, using an autonomous multi-agent Claude Code swarm; marks a qualitative shift in what "AI-assisted science" means.

### 摘要
Claude 的多智能体集群（约60个子智能体）在 Claude Code 中自主运行约1.5天、消耗3100万输出 token 和2400条 shell 命令，将黎曼 ζ 函数非平凡零点位于临界线上的比例下界从41.6%提升至67.2%——近乎翻倍。关键突破在于将临界线上与临界线外的零点作为统一几何空间处理。结果已在 Lean 中形式验证，并经由外部数论学家 Brian Conrey 和 Dan Goldston 独立审核确认。Claude 并未证明黎曼假设本身，但这一进展是该方向数十年来最大的单步突破。

### Summary
An unreleased research Claude ran as a swarm of approximately 60 subagents inside Claude Code, spending roughly 1.5 days, 31 million output tokens, and 2,400 shell commands to improve the proven lower bound for the fraction of Riemann zeta zeros on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2% — nearly doubling a result that had stood since the 1940s. The key insight was treating zeros on and off the critical line as a unified geometric space rather than handling them separately. The proof was formally verified in Lean and independently reviewed by external number theorists Brian Conrey and Dan Goldston. Claude did not prove the Riemann Hypothesis itself, but this is the largest single advance on this bound in decades and the first such result produced by an AI system.

### Main Takeaway
Claude autonomously pushed a 75-year-old mathematical frontier, Lean-verified — AI-assisted science has crossed from "helpful tool" to "independent contributor."

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐⭐⭐ — This result matters to Sunny not as pure mathematics but as credibility evidence and change-management ammunition. When engineering teams push back on the ROI of Claude Code ("it's a fancy autocomplete"), this is the sharpest possible counter-example: an autonomous multi-agent Claude Code swarm produced a formally verified mathematical advance that human researchers had not achieved in decades. It also illustrates the multi-agent architecture (60 subagents, 1.5 days, 2,400 shell commands) that Sunny's teams will eventually be running for complex financial and regulatory analysis tasks. The Lean verification angle is particularly relevant for governance conversations: AI output formally verifiable to the same standard as human-reviewed code.
