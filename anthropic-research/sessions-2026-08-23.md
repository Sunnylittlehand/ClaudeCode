### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ — First rigorous, payment-data-based evidence that enterprise AI pricing has hit a natural ceiling, with direct implications for how Anthropic and OpenAI will compete in the second half of 2026.

### 摘要
Ramp 8 月企业 AI 支出指数揭示关键定价临界点：Anthropic 以 43.5% 的企业采购率领先，但增速放缓；OpenAI 以 39.7% 紧随其后，差距正在缩小。核心问题在于 Fable 5 的价格（约 10 美元/百万 token）是 GPT-5.6 Sol（约 5 美元）的两倍，企业实际使用量远低于预期——Fable 5 仅贡献 Anthropic 6% 的 token 量，而 Sol 占 OpenAI 支出的 25%。Ramp 将此定义为"AI 论题的裂缝"：更高性能不再足以证明更高价格的合理性。

### Summary
Ramp's August 2026 AI Index — drawn from 70,000+ US businesses' actual payment records — shows Anthropic still leads in enterprise adoption at 43.5% vs OpenAI's 39.7%, but OpenAI is growing faster in Q3 2026. The report's headline finding is a *price ceiling*: Anthropic's Fable 5, at ~$10/MTok (twice GPT-5.6 Sol), captured just 6% of Anthropic's tokens and 11.4% of its spend in July, despite being the highest-performing model on the market, while Sol took 25% of OpenAI tokens and 23% of spend. Ramp frames this as "cracks in the AI thesis" — enterprises are signalling that performance beyond a threshold no longer justifies proportionally higher cost. xAI posted its fastest growth since July 2025, reaching 4% of businesses.

### Main Takeaway
For the first time, concrete payment data shows enterprises drawing a price line: Fable 5's performance premium doesn't justify 2× the cost of GPT-5.6 Sol, and that ceiling is reshaping the competitive map.

## [Pew Research: One-Third of New Web Pages Show Signs of AI Authorship](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/)
**Source**: Pew Research Center
**Type**: Research Report
**Date**: 2026-08-20
**Links**: [Primary — Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/a-third-of-webpages-published-since-chatgpts-launch-show-signs-of-ai-authorship-study-finds/)

### Speaker / Author
Pew Research Center — one of the world's most cited nonpartisan research institutions, with a long track record on technology and society studies. This report was produced by its Data Labs division, using Common Crawl web archive data and Open Pangram AI-detection technology. The institutional credibility means this is likely to be cited in regulatory and policy contexts.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐☆☆ — The largest-scale empirical measurement of AI content penetration on the public web to date; establishes a baseline with implications for AI training data quality, content authenticity standards, and regulatory pressure on provenance requirements.

### 摘要
Pew Research 分析约 50 万个英文网页发现：ChatGPT 发布后上线的新页面中，超三分之一（35%）显示 AI 创作迹象；.com 域名占比约 10%，远高于 .edu 或 .gov 的 1%。

### Summary
Pew's analysis of nearly 500,000 English-language web pages (July 2026 Common Crawl sample) found that among pages published after ChatGPT's November 2022 launch, 35% show detectable signs of AI authorship — rising from near zero in 2022 to the current level. Commercial domains (.com) show AI content at roughly ten times the rate of government or academic sites. Pew is careful to note it measures "signs of AI involvement," not fully AI-written pages.

### Relevance to Sunny
⭐⭐☆☆☆ — Indirect relevance for Sunny's rollout. It reinforces the case for provenance and watermarking controls in enterprise AI deployments (supporting Anthropic's August watermarking initiative). It also provides talking-point data for Sunny when change-management conversations arise about AI's broader adoption trajectory — the question is no longer "will staff use AI" but "how do we govern and verify what AI produces."
