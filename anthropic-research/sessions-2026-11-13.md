### Summary

APRA will run scenario-based "AI stress tests" for regulated firms covering extreme scenarios including AI-assisted cyber attacks, model mis-specification, and concentrated reliance on a small number of AI providers. Unusually, the regulatory document explicitly names Anthropic Mythos and OpenAI as specific risk vectors, the first time named AI products have appeared as risks in an APRA/ASIC publication.

### Main Takeaway
