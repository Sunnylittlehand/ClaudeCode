**Type**: Research Announcement / Scientific Discovery

### Speaker / Author

### Summary

Feng Zhang, who helped pioneer CRISPR at MIT and the Broad Institute, reviewed the pre-print and called it "genuinely intriguing" and "an exciting example of how AI agents can contribute to biological discovery." This is the first peer-reviewed result from Anthropic's in-house wet lab and the first publicly documented case of a frontier-model agent swarm making an original biological discovery rather than assisting a human researcher.
