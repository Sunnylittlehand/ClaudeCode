## [Claude Discovers Novel Enzyme System with CRISPR-Like Repeats — First AI-Led Autonomous Biological Discovery](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)
**Source**: Anthropic
**Type**: Research Announcement / Scientific Discovery
**Date**: 2026-09-23
**Links**: [Anthropic announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/anthropic-says-its-biology-lab-has-already-found-something-big/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-claude-enzyme-system-crispr-like-repeats)

### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Biology Research Group. Anthropic formed this internal wet-lab research group in spring 2026 to test whether general-purpose AI agents can systematize and accelerate biological discovery at biosafety levels 1 and 2. This is its first published result.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Landmark demonstration of autonomous multi-agent biological research: 950 Claude agents, 21 hours, 210 million tokens, zero human guidance after the initial prompt, producing a peer-reviewed pre-print endorsed by a CRISPR pioneer.

### 摘要
Anthropic生物实验室宣布Claude自主发现了一种此前未描述的酶系统——"阵列关联逆转录酶"（ART），其结构与CRISPR基因编辑中的DNA重复序列相似。950个Claude智能体在无人工干预下搜索噬菌体数据库，耗时21小时、使用2.1亿tokens，完成了这一发现。MIT/Broad研究所的Feng Zhang评价该发现"真正引人入胜"，称其为"AI智能体助力生物发现的精彩范例"。

### Summary
Anthropic's biology lab published a pre-print in which roughly 950 Claude agents autonomously searched a bacteriophage DNA database, identified a novel enzyme system whose gene is flanked by repeating DNA arrays resembling a CRISPR pattern, and characterised the system — now named array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART). The entire database search required no human guidance beyond the initial prompt; it ran for 21 hours and consumed 210 million tokens. Feng Zhang, who helped pioneer CRISPR at MIT and the Broad Institute, reviewed the pre-print and called it "genuinely intriguing" and "an exciting example of how AI agents can contribute to biological discovery." This is the first peer-reviewed result from Anthropic's in-house wet lab and the first publicly documented case of a frontier-model agent swarm making an original biological discovery rather than assisting a human researcher.

### Main Takeaway
Claude agents just made a genuine biological discovery in 21 hours that would have taken a human team months — autonomous science is no longer theoretical.
