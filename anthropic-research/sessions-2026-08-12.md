### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Editorial. Announced via updated support documentation and confirmed in press reporting. Anthropic's policy team co-ordinated the rollout with the EU AI Act enforcement timeline.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — First major AI lab to ship universal, globally-applied invisible text watermarking on all model outputs; directly driven by Article 50 of the EU AI Act and extended worldwide, not just to EU users.

### 摘要
Anthropic宣布，自2026年8月2日起发布的所有Claude新模型将在全球范围内对生成的文本嵌入不可见的统计水印，并对文件采用C2PA开放标准进行来源认证。水印在复制粘贴后仍可保留，但大幅改写或经其他AI模型重写后可能失效。此举旨在满足欧盟《人工智能法》第50条的透明度要求，但Anthropic将其全球推广，不局限于欧洲用户。Anthropic还计划向第三方提供检测工具。

### Summary
Every Claude model launched on or after August 2, 2026 now embeds an invisible statistical watermark directly into generated text, with the mark persisting through copy-paste operations but degrading if text is substantially rewritten or processed by another AI model. The rollout is global — not limited to EU users — even though the regulatory trigger is Article 50 of the EU AI Act, which requires AI systems to mark generated content so other systems can identify it. Anthropic plans to extend watermarking support to older models and to release third-party detection tools, though no timeline was given for either commitment.

### Main Takeaway
Anthropic has made invisible AI content provenance the default for all Claude outputs worldwide, setting a new industry baseline for transparency compliance ahead of the EU AI Act's full enforcement cycle.
