### 摘要

### Summary
Anthropic is establishing the Public Benefit Fund as a wholly-owned LLC that will receive 5% of the company's shares (subject to shareholder approval), worth approximately $48 billion at the current ~$965B valuation. An additional 2% of shares may be added at board discretion for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline. Combined with the founders' 80% equity pledge and an employee equity-match programme, Anthropic says roughly 20% of shares will be dedicated to public benefit, with more possible via valuation milestones. The Fund's LLC structure (rather than a 501(c)(3)) allows both traditional charitable grants and direct distributions — for example, to populations affected by labour-market disruption, which Anthropic explicitly names as a use case. No lobbying or political giving will be funded. Anthropic says further governance details and a public benefit report are forthcoming.

### Main Takeaway
Anthropic is structurally binding a large fraction of its future equity upside to public-benefit outcomes — including labour-market impact mitigation — with accountability mechanisms that will be visible to public investors once the S-1 is filed.
