### Summary
Anthropic on October 2 launched Claude Frontier Academy, a $100 million programme targeting 10,000 certified Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by end-2027. The inaugural FDE Residency — the programme's flagship track — is a selective, medical-residency-style programme combining multi-day intensive training with a 12-week on-the-job rotation; first cohorts run in San Francisco, New York, and London in October–November 2026. Named first-cohort partners are Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, and Novo Nordisk. Anthropic framed the initiative around a structural enterprise AI talent gap: a small number of deeply skilled practitioners account for a disproportionate share of the value AI delivers inside large organisations, and the Academy is designed to scale that population by an order of magnitude.
