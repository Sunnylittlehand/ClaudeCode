## [Claude Resolves 25-Year-Old NIST ECDSA Curve Mystery — Seeds Traced to 1990s Unix Timestamps in Under 30 Minutes](https://anthropic.com/research/ecdsa-curve-origins)
**Source**: Anthropic Frontier Red Team
**Type**: Research Paper / Security Research
**Date**: 2026-10-16
**Links**: [Anthropic Frontier Red Team post](https://anthropic.com/research/ecdsa-curve-origins) · [AIWeekly coverage](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-claude-traced-nist-ecdsa-seeds-to-unix-timestamps) · [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/claude-traces-nist-ecdsa-seeds-to-timestamps)

### Speaker / Author
Anthropic Frontier Red Team. The team is Anthropic's in-house security and dangerous-capability evaluation unit, which has previously published work on open-weight model cyber capabilities (GLM-5.3, Sep 29), IoT self-replication risk (Oct 12–13), and the Cyber Mission programme. Their work typically sits at the intersection of AI safety and applied cryptography/cybersecurity.

### Significance Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ — Claude solved in under 30 minutes a cryptographic provenance question that stumped cryptographers for 25 years, demonstrating applied research capability with direct implications for how AI will be used in security audits; finding also clarifies (and largely exonerates) the longstanding suspicion of an NSA backdoor in NIST curves.

### 摘要
Anthropic前沿红队2026年10月16日发布研究报告：Claude模型在不到30分钟内追溯了NIST椭圆曲线密码（ECDSA）P-192、P-224、P-256、P-384、P-521曲线参数种子的来源——这是一个困扰密码学界25年的公开悬案。Claude判断生成种子使用的是C标准库lrand48伪随机数生成器，以1990年代Unix时间戳为初始值，例如P-256种子从1997年8月16日01:52:34的Unix时钟读数出发，历经1,842次迭代生成。此发现不构成对现有ECDSA曲线的密码学攻击，但显著降低了"NSA蓄意植入后门"的合理性。B-163曲线种子仍无法解释。用于寻找种子的$36,864奖金将捐赠给互联网安全研究组（ISRG）。

### Summary
Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published a paper on October 16 showing that an internal Claude research model traced the seeds for NIST's elliptic-curve standards (P-192, P-224, P-256, P-384, P-521) to the C library's `lrand48` pseudo-random number generator, seeded with Unix timestamps from the mid-1990s. As an illustrative example: P-256's standardised seed required 1,842 iterations of the generator starting from a Unix clock reading of August 16, 1997, 01:52:34. Claude generated a scanner covering 34 generator-and-output-slice combinations, and the model completed the work in under 30 minutes. Anthropic is explicit that this is not a cryptographic break — the curves remain secure — but the finding makes a deliberately hidden NSA backdoor significantly less plausible, since anyone with 1990s timestamps and the same PRNG could reproduce the seeds independently. One outstanding puzzle: the B-163 curve seed fits none of Claude's tested hypotheses and remains unexplained. The $36,864 provenance bounty (organised by Filippo Valsorda) will be donated to the Internet Security Research Group.

### Main Takeaway
Claude resolved a 25-year-old cryptographic mystery in 30 minutes — and the answer reduces (but does not eliminate) suspicion of a deliberate NSA backdoor in the most widely deployed elliptic curves on the internet.
