### Summary
On August 27, 2026, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and 112 other entities — including Citi, Capital One, Mastercard, Visa, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, and Hugging Face — published a joint open letter warning that "AI-enabled cyber attacks will become far more widespread and sophisticated in the coming months." The letter calls for a coordinated "defensive surge" through new public-private partnerships and raises security standards across critical infrastructure. Published days after OpenAI's technical report on the Hugging Face breach and amid the DseWiki investigation, the letter represents the first time AI labs and major financial institutions have publicly aligned on AI-specific cyber risk. No specific regulatory mandates or binding commitments were included; the signatories frame it as a first step toward defining shared standards.

### Main Takeaway
Major AI labs and financial institutions are now publicly co-signing that AI-enabled attacks are not a future risk — they are an imminent reality requiring immediate coordinated response.
