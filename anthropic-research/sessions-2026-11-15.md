### 摘要
英国金融行为监管局（FCA）于 2026 年 10 月 19 日发布《金融服务业人工智能：良好实践与改进领域》，这是迄今为止 FCA 最详尽的 AI 治理指南。文件不创设新规则，而是将现有法规（消费者责任、SYSC、SM&CR、DISP 及运营韧性框架）映射至 AI 应用场景，并以"良好实践"与"不良实践"对照方式呈现。核心要求包括：高级管理人员须对 AI 系统负可问责的监督责任、不能以外包转移合规责任、必须测试不同消费者群体（含弱势群体）的 AI 输出结果，以及为投诉和可解释性需求保留 AI 提示词、输出和模型版本记录。FCA 还特别提出"代理式 AI"的良好实践——企业应建立在线代理中央台账，列明所有者、访问权限和控制措施，并表示将开发代理式金融的信任框架。

### Summary
The FCA published *AI in Financial Services: Good Practice and Areas for Improvement* on 19 October 2026, its most detailed public articulation of AI governance expectations to date. The paper does not introduce new rules but maps existing obligations (Consumer Duty, SYSC, SM&CR, DISP, operational resilience) onto AI use, illustrating each with concrete good and poor examples drawn from thematic reviews of live deployments. Key good practices: senior managers accountable for AI systems with clear SMF ownership; firms retain accountability for outsourced/vendor AI and cannot accept vendor assurances without testing them; AI outputs tested across consumer groups including vulnerable customers with error-rate comparisons; records kept of consumer prompts, outputs, model versions, applied filters, and escalation decisions to support complaints and explainability. Key poor practices: governance retrofitted after a tool is deployed; human review becoming a rubber-stamp as reviewers increasingly accept AI recommendations without scrutiny; excluding vulnerable consumers from AI journeys without an alternative. For agentic AI specifically, the FCA flags that good practice includes a central live-agent inventory (owner, access, controls), and signals it will develop a trusted framework for agentic finance.

### Main Takeaway
The FCA has told UK financial services firms exactly what it looks for when it examines AI governance — and "we outsourced it" and "we added a human reviewer" are explicitly called out as poor practice.
