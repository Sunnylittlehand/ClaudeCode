### 摘要
Claude Code v2.1.261（9月4日）新增/skill-doctor命令，可识别未使用的技能及其上下文成本；新增bashOutputMaxChars/taskOutputMaxChars上限设置（最多128K字符）；添加--append-subagent-system-prompt-file标志；组织策略无法加载时在/status和claude doctor中显示原因（如代理配置问题）。此外修复了大量Remote Control显示、会话管理及模型选择器问题。

### Summary
v2.1.261 ships four enterprise-relevant additions: the `/skill-doctor` slash command identifies unused installed skills and reports their context-window cost overhead — directly addressing token-budget creep in organisations with many installed skills; `bashOutputMaxChars` and `taskOutputMaxChars` settings let admins raise inline command output limits up to 128K characters before Claude saves output to file; the `--append-subagent-system-prompt-file` flag lets operators inject large system prompts for subagents from files rather than inline; and the Organisation Policy Feedback enhancement shows an explanatory note in `/status` and `claude doctor` when policies cannot be loaded (e.g. due to proxy misconfiguration), replacing the previous silent failure. Multiple Remote Control display staleness bugs, session management edge cases, and model picker human-readable-name improvements are also included.

### Main Takeaway
